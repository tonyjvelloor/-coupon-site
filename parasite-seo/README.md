# CouponHub India — Parasite SEO Master Execution Playbook

## 1. What is Parasite SEO & Why is It Our Highest-ROI Organic Growth Engine?

In competitive affiliate search verticals (such as "Amazon promo codes", "Flipkart coupon code today", "Myntra 500 off voucher"), ranking a new domain (Domain Rating 1–25) on Google Page 1 through standard SEO alone normally requires 6 to 12 months of high-cost link-building and domain aging.

**Parasite SEO flips this dynamic:**
Instead of waiting for Google to build trust in `couponhub.store`, we publish high-intent, authoritative content on third-party platforms that **already have Domain Authority 85–98 and tens of millions of trusted backlinks**. 

When we publish on these authority platforms, Google indexes our articles within 1–4 hours and ranks them on Page 1 for high-volume commercial queries. Each parasite article then funnels targeted, high-intent traffic directly to `couponhub.store` store and tool landing pages.

## 4. The 4-Step Fast Indexing SOP

When you publish any parasite article from this suite:

1. **Self-Ping via IndexNow & Social:**
   - Share the published parasite link on Twitter/X, Reddit, and LinkedIn. Social signals trigger Google's real-time crawler.
2. **Submit to Google Search Console (if verified on the property) or Ping via Google URL Inspection:**
   - For Substack or personal medium custom domains, submit via GSC.
3. **Internal Linking from Existing Parasites:**
   - Link between your parasite assets (e.g., your LinkedIn article links to your Medium post and GitHub repo, and all three link back to `couponhub.store`). This creates a **Parasite Authority Ring**.
4. **Tier-2 Backlink Amplification:**
   - Drop a link to your Medium/LinkedIn post inside a relevant Quora answer or Reddit discussion. Google ranks parasite pages exponentially higher when they receive discussion mentions.
