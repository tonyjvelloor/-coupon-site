### Question 3: "How do instant credit card bank offers work during Amazon Great Indian Festival and Flipkart Big Billion Days?"

**Answer:**

Instant bank discounts are co-funded marketing promotions between the e-commerce retailer and the issuing bank (like HDFC, SBI, ICICI, or Axis).

Here is exactly how they work:
- **Eligibility:** You must pay using the specified bank credit or debit card (often EMI transactions also qualify).
- **Threshold Limit:** You must meet a minimum transaction value (typically ₹4,999 or ₹5,000).
- **Calculation:** The 10% instant discount is deducted immediately on the payment gateway before you enter your OTP. For example, if your cart is ₹10,000, the bill becomes ₹9,000 instantly.
- **Cap on Savings:** There is always a maximum cap (usually ₹1,250 to ₹1,750 on non-EMI, and up to ₹3,000 to ₹5,000 on EMI or high-ticket electronics like laptops and iPhones).
