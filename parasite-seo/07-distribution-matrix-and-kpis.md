## 2. Tracking & Conversion Metrics

To measure the ROI and traffic generated by each parasite asset, use UTM campaign tracking when sharing URLs from your parasite accounts:

### Standard UTM Parameter Template:
- **Medium:** `https://www.couponhub.store/stores/amazon?utm_source=medium&utm_medium=parasite_article&utm_campaign=amazon_deals_2026`
- **LinkedIn:** `https://www.couponhub.store/banks?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=pulse_article&utm_campaign=credit_card_playbook`
- **Reddit:** `https://www.couponhub.store/stores/myntra?utm_source=reddit&utm_medium=community_post&utm_campaign=myntra_hacks`
- **GitHub:** `https://www.couponhub.store?utm_source=github&utm_medium=awesome_repo&utm_campaign=developer_coupons`

### Primary KPIs:
1. **Google SERP Position:** Target parasite page ranking in top 5 on Google for primary keyword within 7–14 days.
2. **Referral Click-Through Rate (CTR):** 10% to 25% of parasite readers clicking through to `couponhub.store`.
3. **Affiliate Link Activations:** Outbound clicks from `couponhub.store/out/[dealId]` to merchant stores.
4. **AdSense Impressions & RPM:** Additional pageviews on `couponhub.store` monetized by Google Auto Ads.
5. **Tier-1 Backlink Authority:** Noticeable increases in Ahrefs/Semrush referring domains and root Domain Rating.
