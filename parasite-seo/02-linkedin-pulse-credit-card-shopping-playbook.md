### The Fundamental Rule: Instant Discount vs. Reward Points

One of the most common questions among reward-point enthusiasts is:

> *"Should I use an instant 10% bank discount card, or swipe my 5% uncapped cashback card?"*

Let's do the math on a ₹50,000 electronics or festive shopping cart:

#### Scenario A: Using a Flat 5% Cashback Card (e.g., SBI Cashback)
- Cart Value: ₹50,000
- Cashback Earned (next statement cycle): **₹2,500**
- Net Cost: ₹47,500

#### Scenario B: Using a Bank Instant Discount Tie-Up (e.g., HDFC 10% Instant Off)
- Cart Value: ₹50,000
- Instant Discount at Checkout: -₹1,500 to ₹3,000 (depending on sale tier)
- New Amount Swiped: ₹47,000
- Milestone Reward Points / Base Reward Rate (typically 1.3% to 3.3%): +₹900 value
- Net Effective Cost: **₹46,100**

**The Key Takeaway:**  
Instant bank discounts always beat delayed cashback because **instant discounts reduce the initial invoice value before GST and taxes**, and you still accrue base reward points or milestone spend progress on the reduced swiped amount.
